About Barakat Arabia

A practical partner for demanding construction projects

Barakat Arabia brings general contracting and construction together with the electromechanical expertise described in the Electrolink profile: supply, installation and maintenance of MEP systems, facility management, project management and technical design.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality solutions, reliable services and superior materials with professionalism and a clear commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

Our Services

One team from pre-construction to handover

01
Procurement & Equipment Selection

Value review and development; value engineering; equipment selection; material submittals; procurement and inspection.

02
Technical Design & Drawings

Engineering calculations; shop drawings, sections and details; schematic and combined services drawings (CSD); as-built record drawings and manuals.

03
Execution & Project Management

Method statements; installation and project management; testing and commissioning; operation and maintenance.

04
Value Engineering

Optimized design solutions; cost control and budget planning; lifecycle cost analysis; constructability and design assessment.

05
Mechanical & Electrical Infrastructure

Coordinated infrastructure engineering, calculations, shop drawings, schematics, combined services drawings, as-built records and manuals.

06
Facility Management & Maintenance

Supply, installation and maintenance of electromechanical systems, with facility management for MEP systems and ongoing operation and maintenance support.
