FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call us on +966 54 412 6721.

What does Barakat Arabia do?

We supply, install and maintain electromechanical (MEP) systems, and provide facility management, project management and technical design — covering the full lifecycle from procurement and shop drawings through installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance.

Which MEP systems do you install?

HVAC; smart power and water metering; solar energy; firefighting; BMS and IoT; plumbing and drainage; electrical lighting and power distribution; communication systems; automation; security and fire alarm; power storage and backup (UPS, battery systems, generators, ATS and synchronisation panels); and facility management for MEP systems.

Where does Barakat Arabia work?

We are based in Saudi Arabia, with project references in Saudi Arabia — including MISK City Mobility Hub and MBS Charity City in Riyadh — as well as Egypt, Kenya and Libya.

What types of projects have you delivered?

Commercial and administrative buildings, pharmaceutical plants and hospitals, industrial facilities, hotels and resorts, residential towers and villas, bank branches, schools, clubs, showrooms and retail.
